Quick Rules

  1. Roll a 6 to move a token out of base.
  2. Tokens move clockwise by the rolled value.
  3. Landing on an enemy token sends it back to base.
  4. Safe or star cells cannot be captured.
  5. Exact roll is required to enter home and win.
